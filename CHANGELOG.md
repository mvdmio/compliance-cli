# Changelog

## 2026-10-12: One CLI for every mvdm.io Product
The command is now `mvdm`. You sign in once with `mvdm login`, and that sign-in works in every Product that offers an API, including one that starts offering it later. Each Product's commands sit under its name, such as `mvdm compliance risks list`, and `mvdm --help` lists the Products you can use. `MVDM_TOKEN` holds a Personal token for all of them.
